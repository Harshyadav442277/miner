 Critical to understand
 How Telegraph Works
 What is an Intent?
 An Intent is a specific category of intelligence that applications can request — for example, AI text detection, weather data, financial signals, or logistics information. Each intent operates with its own independent leaderboard. You only compete with Miners in the same domain for Normalized Performance scoring.
 01
 Miners Are Ranked, Not Just Listed
 Anyone can bring their model, API, dataset, or specialized system into Telegraph as a Miner. Miners are ranked based on performance, domain relevance, and historical quality. Validators continuously evaluate Miner outputs against ground truth using evaluation scripts. Higher and more consistent performance leads to a higher rank on the leaderboard.
 02
 Probabilistic Routing Based on Intent
 When an agent needs intelligence, it does not choose a specific Miner. Instead, it declares its intent — the domain (e.g. AI detection, weather, finance), a minimum confidence threshold, and a deadline. Telegraph routes probabilistically to the top-ranked Miners for that intent. Higher-performing Miners receive more traffic and more USD per query.
 03
 The Quality Flywheel
 Better Miners → more routed demand → more real USD earnings → stronger earnings attract better Miners → network compounds in quality, reliability, and coverage. Fundamentally different from simple aggregator models.
 04
 Independent Leaderboards per Intent
 Each intent (e.g. AI text detection) has its own independent leaderboard. Miners are only ranked against others in the same domain. Miners solving completely different tasks do not affect your ranking or routing share. This ensures fair competition within each domain.
 05
 Why This Architecture Matters
 Simple aggregator approaches lack ranking, verification, probabilistic routing, and the incentive flywheel that turns raw, unverified API output into reliable, monetizable, machine-grade intelligence. Telegraph is designed to become stronger with real usage, not weaker.

 How winners are chosen
 Judging Criteria
 Track 1: Miners Track 2: Script Authors Track 3: Applications 
 75 % Normalized Performance (within Intent) 
 Your average Canonical Score divided by the highest average score achieved inside your specific Intent. The best Miner in every Intent automatically gets full points — ensuring fairness across Intents of different difficulty.
 25 % Engagement & Updates on X 
 Quality, consistency, reach, and meaningful engagement of updates posted on X. Tag @Telegraphprotoc in all update posts.
 How Miner Track Winners Are Selected Winners are determined using Intradomain Normalization to ensure fairness across intents of different difficulty and volume.
 Every Miner is scored out of 100 points:
 75 points — Normalized Performance (your score relative to the best Miner in your specific Intent)
 25 points — X Engagement & Transparency
 Guardrail: An Intent must have at least 3 active Miners and receive at least 100 real requests from Track 3 applications to be eligible for global cash prizes.
 The Top 3 Miners with the highest total normalized scores across all intents win cash prizes. This ensures the best Miner in every intent has a fair chance to win, regardless of how strict or easy their intent's Canonical Script is.

Registered-manifest divergence — read before deploying the generative phrasing
==============================================================================
checked 2026-09-19 against track1-miner/miner.yaml at revision 0074f14

The manifest is public, pinned to IPFS and hashed on-chain, and registration is
effectively immutable (CLAUDE.md hard rule 3). It was NOT edited by this lane.
Two of its published sentences stop being true the moment the phrasing path is
enabled in production, and both are quoted below verbatim.

/classify  (TEXT_CLASSIFICATION), miner.yaml line 958
  "... such as billing, technical or account issue. There is no fixed taxonomy and
   no model: a label wins on lexical relatedness ... and the answer names the
   words that matched so the choice can be checked."

  With a key set:
   - a model DOES decide, choosing among the labels the request supplied;
   - the answer does NOT name the words that matched — the matched-words clause is
     exactly one of the two clauses the champion scores 0.0000 for.

/sentiment  (SENTIMENT_ANALYSIS), miner.yaml line 928
  "... read with a rule-based word list and the VADER method ... No model is
   involved and nothing is fetched. ... The answer names the words that carried
   the label ... Sarcasm, irony and opinion words outside the list are not
   detected."

  With a key set:
   - a model is involved and a network call IS made (api.groq.com);
   - the answer does not name the carrying words;
   - the sarcasm limitation no longer describes the behaviour, and where the two
     readings differ the published verdict is the model's, not the word list's.

This is an integrity question, not a code question, and it is the operator's to
settle. Three options, none of them taken here:

  1. Leave the key unset in production. Everything in this lane is inert, the
     manifest stays true, and the two intents keep scoring 0.
  2. Set the key and accept that the registered description misdescribes two of
     36 routes until a re-registration is possible.
  3. Re-register with a corrected manifest — immutable in practice, and a
     rejection releases the slug (CLAUDE.md hard rule 3), so not a small step.

Note that the bench in this folder measures what the answer scores, not what the
manifest promises, and no bench can settle option 2.

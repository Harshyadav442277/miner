6. What the change fixes (correctness, verified by test)
---------------------------------------------------------
dateWindow, before -> after, on real recorded questions:

  "between January 1, 2023, and December 31, 2023"     null..null -> 2023-01-01..2023-12-31
  "between January 1st, 2023, and December 31st, 2025" null..null -> 2023-01-01..2025-12-31

A comma after the first year made the pair pattern miss; every later pattern
missed too, so the window came back null and the date filter was dropped in
silence on a question that named its years explicitly. 2 of the 22 real
questions (rows 10 and 14) hit this.

searchTopic, before -> after, on real recorded questions:

  row  5  "all that mention quantum computing in the abstract"        -> "quantum computing"
  row  6  "that mention climate change adaptation in the abstract"    -> "climate change adaptation"
  row 10  "published between January 1, 2023, and December 31, 2023,
           that mention decentralized finance in"                     -> "decentralized finance"
  row 20  "with machine learning in the abstract"                     -> "machine learning"

Those strings went to OpenAlex verbatim as `search=`, which ranks the
scaffolding words alongside the subject.

Counterexamples held: "abstract algebra", "all-optical computing" and
"transformer models with attention mechanisms" are unchanged, and a sentence
that merely lists years ("papers on quantum computing, 2023, 2024, and 2025
alike") still returns an unbounded window.

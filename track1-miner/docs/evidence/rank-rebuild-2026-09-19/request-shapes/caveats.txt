CHAMPION PROVENANCE. Each champion was verified byte-identical to the live one
on 2026-09-19 by downloading its wasm_url from /api/wasm?intent=... and hashing:
reg1265 (GAME_RESULT), reg2945 (CURRENCY_EXCHANGE), reg510 wchk_tol45
(WEATHER_CHECK), reg49 (TVL_LOOKUP), reg220 (URL_SCAN) all matched. The
wasm_hash field the API returns is a different digest and matches neither copy;
the BYTES match, and the bytes are what the scorer loads.

THE REFERENCE IS OURS, NOT A CROSSING MINER'S. Each row scores an unreadable
request shape against OUR answer for the shape the route does read. It therefore
says "this shape scores like a different answer to the same question" and
nothing about whether the answer is correct. G114's rule is not met by that
construction and no rank claim is made.

URL_SCAN'S VALIDITY GATE FAILED. proofgate-url-intelligence and
preflight-ssl-verification both cross this intent every epoch and score 0.5575
against EACH OTHER under reg220 - far below the 0.98 floor - so their agreement
licenses nothing. Ours scores 0.9929 against proofgate and 0.9983 against
preflight, which is at least consistent with the 0.84-0.93 band we hold in
ordinary epochs.

TWO FIXES THE CHAMPION DOES NOT REWARD. url=https%3A%2F%2Fexample.com scored
0.999034 BEFORE, although the answer was about https://2fexample.com/ - a host
nobody asked about. location=N/A scored 0.998764 BEFORE, although it reported
the weather in Cairo, North Carolina rather than Cairo, Egypt. reg220 and reg510
are lexical, and both wrong answers share nearly every word with the right one.
Those two are fixed for correctness, not for score.

TVL'S chain DEFECT IS INTERMITTENT. At 05:10 UTC on 2026-09-19,
protocol=Aave V3&chain=ethereum and &chain=eth both returned provider_unavailable
from production. At 05:45 UTC, six consecutive requests returned a per-chain
figure. The mechanism is not in doubt: api.llama.fi/protocol/aave-v3 is
29,489,980 bytes and took 47.6 s from this machine against TVL_TIMEOUT_MS=4000,
so whether it answers depends on DefiLlama's CDN being warm. The fix falls back
to the all-chain total, stated as the all-chain total, so a cold cache costs a
less specific answer instead of the whole epoch.

A BENCH IS A FILTER, NOT A VERDICT (G62). Only a scored epoch settles any of it.

## The pairs

Each row is `{prompt, chosen, rejected, kind, format, source}`; `source` is the dataset URL plus the row id.
The prompt is the question. The chosen answer always states the gold answer. Per-side details are in
`<split>.meta.jsonl`, line for line.

| kind | rejected answer |
|---|---|
| `wrong_entity` | the gold answer of another question of the same type (person, place, date, number, organisation, other), same article when possible |
| `wrong_number_or_date` | the gold figure or year changed by a small step (years ±1–10, counts ±25%, days ±3, months ±2) |
| `nonresponsive` | (SQuAD) a sentence from the same paragraph without the answer, against the sentence with it |
| `contradicted` | (SQuAD) the gold supporting sentence, but the stated answer replaced by a wrong one of the same type |
| `refusal` | "no answer found" or a hedge, in prose or JSON; only ever against a correct answer |
| `off_topic` | the correct answer to a different question |
| `echo` | the question restated with no answer |

No pair sets a refusal against a wrong answer, in either direction.

Formats: `bare`, `sentence`, `supported` (answer plus 1–2 real sentences of the SQuAD paragraph) and `json`
(text key from answer/summary/result, optional confidence, source/sources/citations, status/verdict,
checked_at/as_of, question; random key order; compact, spaced or indented). Citations are Wikipedia URLs of
real SQuAD article titles. Both sides draw format, layout, confidence, citation count and decorations from the
same distribution, without knowing the label. A per-pair coin decides which side is longer. Lead-ins share
their words with echoes and hedges share theirs with refusals, on both sides.

Splits: SQuAD by article, NQ-open by normalised question (sha256 buckets 80/10/10). No normalised question,
article, paragraph or answer string (gold or wrong) is in two splits. The 420 lines of
`holdout/benchmark_questions.txt` (sha256 `603f686b…`) are excluded (1 question matched). Every rendered text
fits 384 tokens with the pinned tokenizer (1 train row dropped). The build is byte-reproducible (checked by
rebuilding and comparing the files).

Counts: train 9,000 / val 900 / test 900. Each kind is 1,285–1,286 in train and 128–129 in val and test.
SQuAD/NQ: 5,333/3,667, 542/358 and 505/395. Cross-format pairs: 67.3% / 68.6% / 67.8%.

Each format field alone (format, inner body, length bucket, key set, keys, confidence, citations) scores
0.488–0.533 on val and test. Same-format length cells in train: 0.526–0.598. Cross-split overlaps of
questions, articles, paragraphs, answer strings, prompts and chosen texts: all 0. Rejected texts shared with
train: 5.2% of val, 6.6% of test (short refusals such as "Unknown."). Benchmark questions in any split: 0.

**The bag-of-words bar is on the five stated-answer kinds (decided 2026-11-15).** Over all seven kinds the
probe scores 0.638 on val and 0.606 on test, above 0.60. By kind it scores refusal 0.98/0.97 and echo 0.73/0.73;
the other five score 0.43–0.60, pooled 0.55/0.51 (`bow_stated_answer_kinds` in `audit.json`). A refusal or an
echo is wrong whatever the question was, and it should lose (ARCHITECTURE A3 rule 1), so an answer-only probe
is right to spot it. The audit therefore gates the pooled five kinds and reports the other two. Hedges with
refusal words on both sides, answer templates filled with "unknown", and refusals that quote the paragraph
did not bring the all-kinds number under the bar. Rebuilds that differ only in a random draw moved val between
0.574 and 0.638, so the number is noisy at n=900.

## Limits

- SQuAD answer spans are sometimes wrong in the dataset (one seen: "round-back" for the arched-back mandolin).
  Spans inside a word are dropped; other label noise is not.
- Answer types come from a wh-word and shape heuristic; some distractors have a different type.
- All rejected answers are made by our templates and perturbations. Real wrong answers from miners may look
  different. The holdout, not this audit, says whether the model generalises.
- NQ-open rows carry no citations (it has no source document).
- Train/val/test are ours; good val/test numbers do not show anything about the network's hidden pairs.

# The displayed bar is stale — the real bar is `champion_margin`, recomputed per evaluation

**Date:** 2026-08-29 · **Source:** `https://devnode.telegraphprotocol.com/api/wasm`, full 1,512-entry
snapshot saved beside this file as `2026-08-29-wasm-registry-snapshot.json`.

## The mistake this corrects

We had been reading each intent's bar off the champion's `eval_score`. That number is the margin the
champion earned **when it was promoted**, against the fixture snapshot of that moment. It is not what
a new candidate is measured against.

The operative bar is the `champion_margin` field inside a **fresh candidate's** eval — the champion
re-scored on today's fixtures. The two diverge wildly:

| intent | stored `eval_score` | TRUE bar (recomputed) | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| WEATHER_FORECAST | 0.5302 | **0.989652** | we thought this was the softest target in the protocol |
| IP_GEOLOCATION | 0.8574 | **0.925038** | our reg 1377 margin 0.8775 looked like a near-win; it is not close |
| CRYPTO_PRICE | 0.9598 | **0.629564** | far softer than it looked |
| STOCK_PRICE | 0.7961 | **0.614703** | softest true bar in the protocol |
| TVL_LOOKUP | 0.7961 | **0.634025** | |
| TEXT_AUTHENTICITY_CHECK | 0.6586 | **0.658612** | unchanged — confirmed twice today by our own regs 1671/1673 |

**WEATHER_FORECAST was never a 0.53 target.** Any plan built on the stored column was built on sand.

## The bar also drifts, because the fixtures are resampled

Successive probes on one intent see different champion margins:

- STOCK_PRICE: 0.555662 → 0.551831 → 0.614703 (28 Aug 05:50 → 29 Aug 08:06)
- TVL_LOOKUP: 0.510789 → 0.561597 → 0.581738 → 0.634025
- CRYPTO_PRICE: 0.608251 → 0.629564

So the bar must be re-read immediately before signing, and a candidate needs headroom, not a
hairline win.

## The third gate: real-traffic agreement

Two CRYPTO_PRICE candidates **beat the champion on both published axes and were still rejected**:

| reg | cand margin | cand wins | champ margin | champ wins | hist rows | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1586 | 0.620177 | 14/15 | 0.608251 | 14 | 74 | rejected — "disagreed with the champion on real traffic" |
| 1619 | **0.689036** | **14/15** | 0.629564 | 14 | 80 | rejected — same |

That is the Spearman gate, and on an intent with historical traffic it is lethal regardless of
fixture performance. **`historical_rows_evaluated: 0` is therefore a first-class selection criterion.**

## Two more traps seen in live rejections

- **Time budget.** IP_GEOLOCATION reg 1683 and four ONCHAIN_TX_LOOKUP attempts died with
  `evaluation exceeded its time budget (10m)`. A ~24 MB MiniLM module running three embeddings per
  call across fixtures plus up to 86 historical rows is squarely in that danger zone. Our 30 KB
  module's microsecond scoring is a real asset here, not a limitation.
- **Oversized input.** TVL_LOOKUP regs 1587 and 1681 both errored with
  `miner_answer too large` on the same row (miner `optivis-tvl`) — after otherwise beating the
  champion. Any module we ship must survive an oversized answer.

## What our own two data points say about fit

Node-measured, on the node's own fixtures:

| intent | kind | our margin | our wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| IP_GEOLOCATION (reg 1377) | numeric facts | **0.87751794** | 14/15 |
| TEXT_AUTHENTICITY_CHECK (regs 1671/1673) | semantic verdict | 0.3274 / 0.2702 | 9/15, 8/15 |

**Our scorer is strong where answers carry typed numeric facts and weak where they carry a semantic
verdict.** It already produced 0.8775 on a numeric intent — comfortably above the 0.61–0.63 true
bars of STOCK_PRICE, CRYPTO_PRICE and TVL_LOOKUP. We spent two days attacking the one target shape
our architecture is worst at.

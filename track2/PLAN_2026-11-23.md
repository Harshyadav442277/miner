## 2. Why we performed poorly: six causes, in order of cost

1. **We gated against the wrong target.** Our gate (ARCHITECTURE A7, 2026-11-15) required a holdout of real
   miner answers and an identity probe ≤ 0.65.

   The network decides on something else. The current scoring docs (`/docs/scoring/train-a-scoring-adapter`,
   updated 2026-11-22, read today) give the sequence:
   1. 15 public pairs go to our runnable;
   2. the adapter must order at least 70% of them (11 of 15);
   3. the node re-scores the same 30 texts bit for bit;
   4. the adapter competes on the intent's hidden evaluation pool, about 30 pairs drawn from contributor rows;
   5. to take the slot, it must score at least 70% and beat the incumbent by 2 points.

   Real miner answers in our families were identity-confounded (G44–G46, G52), so our gate could not pass. It
   measured the miners, not our scorer. Nine lanes closed before or at training (sanctions, URL Scan,
   SECURITY_REVIEW, EVENT_OUTCOME, QA ×2, EMAIL_SECURITY, WEATHER, VULN, ROUTE, plus the VENDOR and CONTRACT pilots).
2. **We picked families whose truth lives outside the text.** QA facts, DNS records, NVD scores and weather
   observations all live outside the text. A ModernBERT-base reward head with a rank-8 LoRA holds none of them. QA
   v1 and v2 reached 42–43% against a 45% champion (G42, G47).

   The outside champions picked families where the prompt carries the truth: smart-contract code, ticket
   transcripts and patches.
3. **We had no network feedback loop.** The 15 public pairs are seen only by the executor, during the remote
   check. Truvian's SMART_CONTRACT_AUDIT entry (public README, `mrnetwork0001/Truvian`, `evaluator/adapter/`; methods
   read, nothing copied) registered three times in about 17 hours:

   | Version | Public pairs ordered | Next step |
   |---|---|---|
   | v1 | 10/15 | regenerated data from the shapes the check showed |
   | v2 | 8/15 | regenerated again |
   | v3 | 13/15 | canonical at 83.3% on 30 hidden pairs |

   They never trained on the captured texts. We registered one adapter (#46, 7/15) and stopped.
4. **Comparators cannot win on quality.** An exact comparator reproduces the network rule, so every one ties, and
   the newest identical registration takes the slot (G49 rotation, observed again today). Half of our
   registrations went here. They are good validity evidence for the write-up and worth no further time.
5. **The five 422 rejections are a module-kind mismatch** (inferred, G53). Every author's comparator in those
   families is refused the same way: `0xb3fb…` gets "not an integer", others get "must be an integer". Meanwhile
   adapters in LIQUIDITY_DEPTH_VERIFY and MINING_HASHPRICE_VERIFY are judged on text "chosen" pairs. The node sends
   these families text pairs, so a comparator is the wrong module there. Our endpoint logs from 2026-11-17 were not
   read; they are probably past Vercel's log retention.
6. **We never stood up a stable executor host** (G39). Across the registry, the most common rejection is "remote
   executor unreachable" (expired trycloudflare and zkasuran hosts). Any adapter we register needs a host that stays
   up through all three evaluation attempts.

What was not poor, and goes in the submission as validity and docs evidence:
- the comparators are exact on 234,848 node-runtime cases;
- the measurement findings: verity reports NXDOMAIN as success (G46), 12 miners serve CNA instead of NVD (G45),
  and wrong-reference DNS tables (G50);
- the tutorial and the demo video.

## 3. What changes: method (ARCHITECTURE A7, amended today)

A real-answer holdout stays as a **reported** result where one exists. The gate for registration becomes:

1. **Labels correct by construction from the prompt.** The better answer is derived from text the prompt
   carries: contract clauses, the passage to moderate, the patch and its tests. No world fact may decide a pair.
2. **The kit's dataset gate passes** on the generated data: length shortcut ≤ 65%, bag-of-words probe ≤ 65%, at
   least 80% unique worse answers, held-out leak ≤ 10%.
3. **A held-out set from a different seed and different source documents**, plus an out-of-distribution shape
   probe, scored in the node runtime (`harness/measure.py`). The probe uses prose, full JSON, thin JSON, a bare
   verdict and a refusal. Bar: ≥ 85% held-out and ≥ 70% on every shape.
4. **The network's 15-public-pair check is the external gate.** The executor logs each public pair's texts.
   - We use them only to name the *kinds* of pair the pool holds; never as training rows or near-copies.
   - Each next version is generated, not copied. The disclosure says so.
5. **Two registrations per lane at most.** A lane stops if v2 orders fewer than 9 of 15.

Unchanged: no miner fingerprints, LiveCert flagged as a related party, and no training on any row we uploaded to a
pool (A4).
